# Session Plan: Session 5 — The Grand Archive

**Location:** Plane of Eternal Craft (Metallos) → The Fractured Nexus → The Grand Archive
**Primary Goal:** Clear the Molten Veins, reforge the Crimson Thread, and retrieve the **Spindle of Telara (Loom Shard)** from the Grand Archive.

---

## **Scene I: Subterranean Sabotage (The Molten Veins)**

*   **Setting:** The dark, steam-choked subterranean conduits beneath Metallos. Giant brass gears turn slowly in the dark, and rivers of molten metal glow orange, casting long, dancing shadows.
*   **The Situation:** The party descends the elevator shaft into the core of the Molten Veins with **Warden-Captain Jaxen Vance**. The air is heavy, sulfurous, and hot. The **Cult of the Broken Thread** has barricaded the central control dais, attempting to siphon the forge's elemental energy into a void rift.
*   **The Infiltrator:** **[[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Garrick the Frayed\|Garrick the Frayed]]**, a senior forge-wright corrupted by the Cult of the Broken Thread, stands at the central console. He is using void-filaments to unravel the stabilizing gears of the forge.
*   *
    *   **Notable Adversaries & Planar Forces:** [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Garrick the Frayed\|Garrick the Frayed]] and 2 Hellchain Weavers
    *   **Tactical Mechanics (Molten Jets & Shifting Gears):**
        *   On initiative count 20, a pressurized pipe ruptures. Roll a d4 for direction. A 10-foot wide line of superheated steam or molten metal sprays out. Creatures in the area must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **11 (2d10) fire damage** on a failure, or half as much on a success.
        *   The central platforms are giant turning gears. As a bonus action, a creature can manipulate a lever (DC 12 Sleight of Hand or Athletics) to rotate a gear, moving any creatures standing on it by 90 degrees and separating them or breaking line-of-sight.
*   **Resolution:** When Garrick is defeated, he gasps, *"The Loom is a cage... we only wanted to melt the bars..."* before dissolving into grey ash. The conduits are stabilized, and the valves groan as the normal, healthy golden energy flow to the Colossal Forge is restored.

---

## **Scene II: Reforging the Crimson Thread**

*   **Setting:** The chamber of the **Colossal Forge**. A towering, divine forge burning with a roaring, golden-white eternal flame.
*   **The Ritual:** The party, escorted by **Arbiter Valerie Sterling** and witnessed by the Triumvirate, places the tainted **Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave (The Crimson Thread)** onto the **Eternal Anvil**.
*   **Reforging Process:** 
    *   To purge the shadow-magic of Strahd, the forge must be run at maximum heat. The party must assist Valerie in the hammer-work.
    *   **Reforging Checks:** A player (preferably Daine or another martial/crafting character) must make a series of checks: a **a successful effort** to strike the anvil with perfect timing, and a **DC 15 Arcana or Nature check** (assisted by the others) to channel the planar fire.
    *   *Failure:* On a failed check, the player takes 2d6 fire damage from spark-backlash, but the ritual can still succeed with a higher toll.
*   **The Result:** The mists and blood-scent of Barovia burn away in a flash of blinding crimson-gold light. The Crimson Thread is purified, glowing with a fierce, stable warmth. 
*   **The Resonance Hum (The Transition):** As soon as the fragment is purified, it releases a high-pitched, vibrating frequency. The party's **Loom Compass** flares with a bright violet beam, pointing directly back toward the Scholar's District of the **Fractured Nexus**. The mended thread is calling to its sibling: **The Spindle of Telara**.

---

## **Scene III: Reunion with Hook (The Next Stop)**

*   **Arrival:** The party travels back through the planar portal to the **Fractured Nexus**, landing in the **Rift Market**. The city is tense, the "Song of the Loom" vibrating at a new, higher pitch.
*   **The Meeting:** They head directly to **The Frayed End Tavern** as instructed. Sitting at a corner table overflowing with shifting maps, inkpots, and brass drafting tools is their old friend **Hook** (the eccentric Changeling scholar).
*   **Hook’s Story:** Hook is ecstatic to see them. He reveals that after the party stabilized the Crucible (in Session 2), the Pale Lodge was so impressed by the maps he recorded that **Aelira** drafted him as an official field researcher. He has been brought to the Nexus Spire.
*   **The Lead:** Hook has been analyzing the resonance of the Crimson Thread using his instruments.
    *   It points directly to a hidden planar gateway inside the Nexus that leads to **The Grand Archive** (the Vault of Infinite Knowledge).
    *   According to his maps, the next shard—**The Spindle of Telara**—is kept inside the Archive's **Loomweaver's Atrium**.
    *   **The Catch:** The Archive is maintained by **The Librarian**, a strictly neutral eldritch entity. The Lodge cannot storm it; the party must enter legally by paying the **Tithe of Secrets**.
*   **Hook’s Role:** Hook will accompany the party. His maps can help navigate the Archive's "shifting geometry," which rearranges itself to trap thieves.

---

## **Scene IV: The Arcanum Gate & The Tithe**

*   **Location:** The Scholar's District of the Nexus. A massive archway of floating white stone books, glowing with golden runes—the **Arcanum Gate**.
*   **The Gatekeeper:** A silent, towering spectral scribe (an assistant of the Librarian) guards the gate.
*   **The Tithe of Secrets:** To pass, the party must pay the Gatekeeper:
    1.  **The Minor Tithe (Entry):** Each player must step forward and whisper a personal secret or surrender a memory to the gate. (See *DM Notes* for mechanics and roleplay prompts). The secret is drawn out as a glowing blue crystal memory orb.
    2.  **The Major Tithe (Deep Vault Access):** To enter the deep stacks where the Loomweaver's Atrium is located, they must offer knowledge the Archive does not possess. Hook prompts them to offer the **Chrono-Echo memory of the Silent Spindle's Betrayal** which they witnessed in Session 2. The Gatekeeper accepts it, the runes shifting to blue, allowing them through.

---

## **Scene V: The Shifting Labyrinth (The Infinite Stacks)**

*   **Setting:** Endless hallways of dark mahogany bookshelves that stretch infinitely up and down. Books hover, self-sorting. The scent of vanilla, old paper, and dry leather fills the air.
*   **The Hazard: Shifting Geometry:** Every 10 minutes, the bookshelves slide and rotate. Characters must stick close to Hook.
*   **The Ambush:** The **Cult of the Broken Thread (The Entropic Hand)** has infiltrated this level. Led by a Fanatic named **Scribe Kaelen**, they intend to destroy the Spindle to prevent the Loom from being mended.
*   *
    *   **Notable Adversaries & Planar Forces:** 1x Cult Fanatic, 4x Cultists, 2x Void-Wracked Gibbering Mouthers (beasts of raw chaos).
    *   **Tactical Mechanic:** On initiative count 20, the maps shift. Line of sight is broken by sliding bookshelves, or characters are separated. A character can use an action to make a DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check to navigate back to the group using Hook's directions.

---

## **Scene VI: The Loomweaver's Atrium & The Guardian**

*   **Description:** A silent, circular cathedral of white marble. In the center, suspended inside a cage of golden filaments, is the **Spindle of Telara**.
*   **The Guardian: Grisgol **
    *   As they approach, loose parchment, scrolls, and spellbooks fly off the shelves, swarming together to form a giant humanoid construct of paper and ink.
*   **Resolution:** When the Golem is defeated, it collapses into a harmless pile of blank paper. The golden cage opens, allowing them to claim the **Spindle of Telara**.
*   **Hook's Warning:** As they take the shard, Hook notices the "black threads" on their Loom-marks are growing larger. *"The Spindle is pure... but why is it reacting to your marks this way? Something is wrong with the Lodge's tuning."*

## The Factions & Underworld of Metallos

### **The Minor Tithe: Surrendered Secrets**
When players surrender a memory or secret, have them choose one of the following options or write their own. This represents their character "forgetting" or losing access to a piece of themselves:
*   **Memory of a Friend/Ally:** The player loses the memory of a minor NPC they liked. They have disadvantage on Charisma checks against characters from their backstory for the rest of the adventure.
*   **A Combat Technique:** The player "forgets" how to perform a specific combat maneuver or spell. They lose one spell slot of their highest level or have -2 to damage rolls until their next long rest.
*   **A Dark Secret:** Surrendering their darkest secret. The Librarian records it, but the weight is lifted. The player gains the *Inspiration* mechanic immediately, but the Cult of the Broken Thread may find this secret orb later in the campaign.

### **The Spindle of Telara (Loom Shard)**
*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement by a Weave-Touched)*
*   **Passive:** The wearer has advantage on saving throws against spells from the school of Illusion and Divination. You can cast *Comprehend Languages* at will.
*   **Active (1/Day): Rewind Thread.** As a reaction when a creature you can see within 30 feet fails a saving throw or is hit by an attack, you can force the die to be rerolled. The target must use the new roll.
*   **Resonance Hum:** While within 1 mile of another Loom shard, the Spindle vibrates, granting the bearer +2 to passive Perception but causing a constant, dull headache.
