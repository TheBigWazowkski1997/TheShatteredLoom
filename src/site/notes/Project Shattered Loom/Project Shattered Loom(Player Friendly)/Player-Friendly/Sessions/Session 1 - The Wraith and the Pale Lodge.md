# Chronicle: Session 1 - The Wraith and the Pale Lodge

## **Scene I: Dream of the Loom**

**Tone:** Surreal, foreboding, prophetic. 
> Darkness folds in on itself. You find yourself standing in a vast void of silver threads, stretching endlessly in all directions. A figure — tall, cloaked in shadows — sits before a great loom made of bone and starlight.
> 
> Its fingers weave strands of light and shadow together. Each thread hums with a familiar pulse — your heartbeat. When the figure turns toward you, its face is a reflection of your own.
> 
> “All threads must return to the weave,” it whispers. “Yours have been chosen. Seek the Pale Lodge. Remember what you were.”

Each player wakes from the dream, hearing the whisper echo faintly in their thoughts. The mark on their skin burns faintly with cold light before fading. They are aboard **The Wraith**.

---

## **Scene II: Aboard the Wraith**

**Setting:** The Spelljammer Wraith glides through the Astral Sea. **Tone:** Cold majesty, isolation, unease.
**Description:**

> The ship creaks like a beast asleep. Its hull is black glass, etched with faint runes that shimmer like veins of quicksilver. Above, the Astral Sea stretches infinite — an ocean of silver mist and drifting fragments of forgotten worlds. The only sound is the slow heartbeat of the ship’s core crystal, pulsing like a dying star.

**Key NPCs:**

* **Captain Seris Vael**, a grim githyanki woman with silver eyes and a voice like rusted steel. Once a raider, now a hired pilot for the Pale Lodge.
* **Quartermaster Irieth**, a mute elven psion whose telepathy feels like cold silk in the mind. She silently checks the party’s “stabilization wards,” ensuring they don’t drift apart in the psychic tides.
* **Crew of five**, each bearing faint astral scars — evidence of past exposure to things that should not be remembered.

**Exploration Options:**

* Explore the ship (they may find a crystal navigation chart with moving constellations).
* Speak with the Captain (she mentions the Pale Lodge lies “beyond the Veins of Thought”).
* Perception or Arcana check: notice faint flickers of something moving just beyond sight — as though something large is _watching the ship through the Sea itself._

---

## **Scene III: The Starved Mind **

**Description:**

> A shadow passes over the Wraith — but there is no sun here. The light bends, the air hums. The skull of a slain god drifts ahead, its hollow eyes glowing faintly with psychic light. Then, a voice claws into your mind:
> 
> _“Thoughts so bright. So hungry. Let me taste them…”_
> 
> From behind the skull, an Astral Skiff emerges — jagged, alive, its hull pulsating like a muscle. Upon it stands a Mind Flayer, draped in tattered robes of constellations. Its tentacles writhe in anticipation.

**Notable Adversaries & Planar Forces:**

* **Xeth’Lorr the Starved ** — weakened but desperate.
	* **Psionic Hunger :** When a creature fails an INT save near him, they lose a memory .
	* **Astral Pulse :** A 20ft psychic gravity well pulling creatures toward him.
	* * **Thralls of the Forgotten (3–5)**
	* Appear as desiccated humanoids with glowing crystals lodged in their skulls.
	* **Unique flavor:** When slain, they burst into fragments of memory (the PCs see flashes of the lives they once lived).

**Environment Hazards:**

* **Gravity Drift:** Creatures knocked prone may float away unless anchored.
* **Debris Fields:** Provide cover or impale floating bodies.
* **Ship’s Harpoon:** Can impale the Skiff if used cleverly (DC 14 check).

**Resolution:**

* If defeated, Xeth’Lorr’s mind collapses inward, leaving behind a black shard of crystallized thought.
	* **Arcana Check (DC 15):** Reveals it hums with the same resonance as their Loom mark.
	* Touching it grants a shared vision — the Pale Lodge calling to them through the silver void.

**Rewards:**

* 300–400 XP per player (or equivalent).
* _Fragment of the Starved_ — a minor magic item that glows when psionics are near.

## **Scene IV: Through the Silver Veins**

**Tone:** Awe and quiet after chaos.

> The Wraith sails on, wounded but unbroken. The Astral Sea begins to thin into shimmering filaments — rivers of light that twist and flow like veins through glass. Shapes drift past: whales of thought, fragments of memories, and glimpses of worlds long devoured by time.
> 
> Then, on the horizon, something stirs — a light not of stars but of reflection.

**Transition:**

* A ghostly choir hums in the distance.
* The pale glow of the **Pale Expanse** begins to bloom, and within it: a colossal structure suspended in the void.

---

**NPC:**

* **Elyndra Vath**, Keeper of the Pale Loom.
	* Her body seems half-real, half-remembered. Her eyes hold galaxies. Her voice echoes directly in the mind:
		“Welcome, Weave-Touched. You carry threads yet unwoven. The Loom has called you… and so you must listen.”

## Scene V: Arrival at the Pale Lodge

_(unchanged in setup — see previous version for arrival and lore)_
After their audience with Elyndra Vath in the **Atrium of Reflection**, the Loom pulses one last time. Threads of silver light shoot outward, weaving together into a constellation-like map that hangs in midair before the adventurers.

> Elyndra gestures to the center of the map — a radiant knot of gold and black threads. “This is the Crucible Realm — the Material Plane as mortals once knew it. But now it churns like molten glass. The Loom’s first shard fell there when the Weave broke.”

The threads twist and flicker, showing visions of strange landscapes — volcanoes spewing liquid fire that hardens into obsidian glass, forests glowing with Feywild luminescence, and oceans that spiral into the heavens.

> “You must find the first Shard of the Loom there — before the planes tear what remains of it apart.”

---

## Scene VI: Mission Briefing — The Shard in the Crucible

Elyndra leads the heroes to a circular platform carved from transparent stone. At its center is a pool of liquid starlight — a **planar portal nexus** that shifts like a living mirror.

> “The shard’s descent has warped the land. We call it the **Crucible Realm**, where the elements bleed and the veils grow thin. The first Loom Shard fell near a _Shatterpoint_ — a wound between realms where raw creation seeps through. You will know it by the hum in your mark.”

She places a faintly glowing crystal in their hands — the **Loom Compass**, now attuned to the Crucible’s energy.
**Loom Compass (Wondrous Item, Uncommon)**

* _Activation:_ As an action, the bearer can sense the direction of the nearest Loom Shard within one mile.
* _Planar Pulse:_ Once per long rest, the compass can stabilize a Wild Magic surge or suppress planar chaos for 1 minute.

> “The shard’s energy calls to beings that crave power — creatures twisted by the fusion of realms. Beware them. And beware the world itself — the Crucible is alive, reshaping itself to test the will of those who walk upon it.”

## Scene VII: Departure — Through the Loom Gate

**Description:**

> The chamber darkens as Elyndra raises her staff. The pool of light ripples, forming a vortex of mirrored reality. Through it, you glimpse impossible landscapes — fire seas, crystal jungles, mountains that float against gravity.
> 
> “When you step through,” she says, “the Wraith will find you again when the stars align. Until then, your threads are your own to weave.”

Each adventurer feels their Loom mark thrum like a heartbeat. The portal’s pull is irresistible — like gravity made of light.
**Transition Moment (Optional flavor):**

> As they pass through, they glimpse flashes of themselves — older, younger, broken, triumphant — infinite possibilities unraveling before coalescing into _now_.

## Scene VIII: Arrival in the Crucible Realm — The Material Plane Reforged

> You awaken on a cliff overlooking a valley unlike any world you’ve known.
> 
> The sky burns with twin suns — one real, one reflected — and the air hums with wild energy. Rivers of molten glass carve through verdant jungle; crystals grow from trees like fruit; in the distance, mountains float, bound to the ground by chains of lightning.
> 
> Magic saturates the air — every breath tastes like ozone and promise.

**Environmental Traits:**
**Wild Magic Currents:** Each dawn, there’s a 10% chance per day that a random spell effect erupts nearby — beneficial, bizarre, or catastrophic.

**Planar Bleed:** Areas where other planes touch the Crucible. For example:

* A grove where Fey creatures whisper through petals of glass.
* A canyon filled with shadows that stretch and move independently.
* A volcano whose lava glows blue and sings when it cools.

## Adventure: **The Scholar’s Journal**

**Recommended Party Level:** 5–7 (scales easily) **Themes:** Forbidden knowledge, planar instability, fire that remembers, obsession **Location:** A town near a thinning of the planes (name as needed)

---

## ACT I — The Eccentric Scholar

### NPC: **Hook**

_Changeling Cartographer of the Pale Lodge (Unaffiliated)_
**Appearance & Mannerisms**

* Rarely keeps the same face for long; changes subtly mid-conversation.
* Fingers constantly smudged with charcoal, ash, and strange red ink.
* Speaks in excited half-sentences, often correcting himself aloud.
* His maps _hum softly_ when unfolded near magical effects.

**Motivation** Hook believes that if she maps the Loom itself—or even one fragment—she will be _seen_ by Telara and invited into the Pale Lodge. she is not ambitious for power, only **recognition**.
**Hook for the Party** Hook seeks protection while she confirms his latest discovery. sHe has found **“energy currents”** beneath the town that do not match ley lines, elemental flows, or known planar faults.

> “These currents don’t move like magic. They _tug_. Like thread under tension.”

### The Journal

Hook provides **The Scholar’s Journal**, containing:

* Hand-drawn maps overlaid with glowing ember-red filaments.
* Notes describing _heat without flame_ and _fire that whispers_.
* Repeated references to a **resonant anchor**—the Loom fragment.

When the journal is opened near spellcasting, the pages briefly scorch but do not burn.

---

## ACT II — The Planar Bleed

Following Hook’s maps leads the party to an abandoned structure, mine, or ruin where the Material Plane has begun to **overlap with the Elemental Plane of Fire**.

### Environmental Effects (Use 1–3 at a time)

* **Ashfall:** Light ash drifts constantly; Perception checks rely on sound instead of sight.
* **Untrue Flame:** Fire sheds dim light but does not warm; warmth comes from the air itself.
* **Reality Scars:** Cracks in the air leak embers upward like reverse rain.
* **Heat Memory:** Creatures that take fire damage briefly hear whispered voices describing past fires.

### Minor Encounters

* **Cinder Wisps:** Fire elementals that resemble drifting thoughts.
* **Fire-Warped Creatures:** Animals partially converted into living embers.
* **Loom Resonance Pulses:** Magical effects surge unpredictably (spells may deal +1 fire damage or cause glowing afterimages).

Hook refuses to go further once the air itself begins to burn—he _knows_ the Loom fragment is close.

---

## ACT III — The Loom Fragment Chamber

At the heart of the planar bleed lies a **twisted nexus**, half obsidian cavern, half living inferno.

* A **Loom Fragment** is suspended in the air, unraveling and re-weaving threads of flame and reality.
* Each thread connects to the environment like veins.
* The fragment hums in the same rhythm as Hook’s maps.

Guarding it is something drawn not merely by fire—but by **resonance**.

---

## The Darkfire Dragon

### Description

The **Darkfire Dragon** is not flesh and scale—it is **fire given will**.
Its body is composed of living black-red flame, edges flickering like torn cloth. Within its chest burns a **cold star of shadowed fire**, and when it exhales, the flames leave _afterimages_, as if reality struggles to remember them.
Its wings do not beat—they _unfold_, stretching the air into glowing fractures. Wherever it steps, the ground briefly ignites, then turns to glass.
The dragon does not roar—it **crackles**, its voice layered with the sound of burning libraries, collapsing forges, and dying suns.
This creature was drawn here because the Loom Fragment **sings in fire’s native tongue**.

---

## Darkfire Dragon (D&D 5e Stat Block)

**Large elemental, chaotic neutral** **Armor Class** 16 (natural armor) **Hit Points** 168 (16d10 + 80) **Speed** 40 ft., fly 80 ft. (hover)
**STR** 20 (+5) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 20 (+5) **INT** 12 (+1) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 16 (+3)
**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Con +9, Wis +6 **Damage Immunities** fire, poison **Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks **Condition Immunities** poisoned, charmed, frightened **Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 12 **Languages** Ignan, Draconic, understands Primordial **Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

---

### Traits

**Living Conflagration** A creature that hits the Darkfire Dragon with a melee attack while within 5 feet takes **5 (1d10) fire damage**.
**Planar Resonance** While within 60 feet of the Loom Fragment, the dragon gains advantage on saving throws and its fire damage ignores resistance.
**Darkfire Nature** Fire damage dealt by the dragon cannot ignite objects unless the dragon chooses.

---

### Actions

**Multiattack** The dragon makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.
**Bite** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target Hit: 15 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) fire damage
**Claw** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) fire damage
**Darkfire Breath (Recharge 5–6)** The dragon exhales blackened flame in a 30-foot cone. Creatures in the area make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw.

* Fail: 45 (10d8) fire damage and are **seared** (disadvantage on the next saving throw they make).
* Success: Half damage, no seared effect.

---

### Optional Mythic Twist (If You Want Stakes Higher)

If the Loom Fragment is damaged or removed mid-fight:

* The dragon gains **one extra attack per round**, but begins to destabilize.
* On death, it collapses into a **mini firestorm**, forcing Dex saves to escape.

## ACT IV — Aftermath & Consequences

### If the Party Claims the Loom Fragment

* The planar bleed begins to close… slowly.
* Fire in the region becomes _unnatural_ for weeks.
* Hook records everything feverishly—and sends copies to the Pale Lodge.

### Hook’s Fate

* If he survives: he becomes a **recurring NPC**, closer than ever to being noticed.
* If he dies: his journal becomes a **relic**, and Telara _does_ notice—just not kindly.

### Pale Lodge Reaction

This fragment confirms:

* The Loom can **anchor elemental realities**
* Some guardians are not constructed—but **drawn**

Telara will want to know **what else is listening**.

# Side Quest: **The Missing Expedition**

**Recommended Party Level:** 4–7 **Placement:** Before _or_ after Ashera’s Rest **Tone:** Quiet dread, academic ruin, things that should not converge

---

## QUEST HOOK — The Pale Lodge’s Silence

The Pale Lodge requests the party’s aid—**officially** framed as a _recovery mission_, but the subtext is clear: **an expedition has gone missing**, and the Lodge cannot afford public knowledge of it.

### Briefing NPC: **Archivist Merrow**

A senior Lodge functionary, calm to the point of discomfort.

> “The Convergence Temple was… theoretically stable. The silence was unexpected.”

The expedition was sent to investigate a site where **multiple planar energies overlap**, not unlike—but older and more _deliberate_ than—Ashera’s Rest.

---

## THE EXPEDITION (Missing)

The party receives partial records of the lost team:

1207. **Arcanist Vale Corryn** – Specialist in Loom harmonics
1208. **Warden Elth Korr** – Martial escort, oathbound to the Lodge
1209. **Initiate Saela** – Scribe and observer, newly marked by the Loom
1210. **Guide “Moth”** – Local tracker, not Lodge-affiliated

Only one message was received before contact was lost:

> “The Temple is not ruined. It is **paused**.”

## ACT I — The Road to the Convergence Temple

The journey itself foreshadows what lies ahead.

### Signs Along the Way

* Compass needles spin, then snap to stillness.
* Campfires burn with mixed colors (blue, gold, ember-red).
* Spells cast for navigation or detection produce **overlapping results**.

If the party has already been to Ashera’s Rest:

* They feel **recognition**, as though the Loom hums faintly in the distance.

---

## ACT II — The Convergence Temple

### Description

The Convergence Temple stands intact—too intact.

* Stone walls shimmer between materials: obsidian, marble, crystal, bone.
* Elemental symbols overlap incorrectly.
* The air smells of rain, ash, ozone, and incense _simultaneously_.

This place was not built for worship—it was built to **observe convergence**.

---

## ACT III — What Went Wrong

Inside the temple, the party finds the expedition’s fate **fragmented across planes**.

### Discoveries

**Arcanist Corryn**

* Half-phased into the wall, alive but trapped.
* Speaks in overlapping voices when freed.
* Reveals they found a **false Loom echo**—a convergence without a fragment.

**Initiate Saela**

* Her notes are scattered and partially burned.
* Last entry:
	“The Loom does not bind this place. It listens.”
	

**Warden Elth Korr**

* Found guarding nothing.
* His body stands, animated by conflicting elemental oaths.
* He attacks only if approached with weapons drawn.

**Guide Moth**

* Missing entirely.
* Signs suggest they were **pulled through**, not killed.

## ACT V — Choices & Consequences

### Option 1: Stabilize the Temple

* Requires Arcana, Religion, or Loom-mark resonance.
* Prevents further disappearances.
* The Lodge gains trust—but loses access to deeper secrets.

### Option 2: Extract Knowledge

* Risk destabilizing the site further.
* Gain partial Loom insight (foreshadowing future fragments).
* May strand someone permanently between planes.

### Option 3: Collapse the Convergence

* Safest long-term.
* Moth is likely lost forever.
* The Lodge marks this site as **forbidden**.
