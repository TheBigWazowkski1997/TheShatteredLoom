# Count Strahd von Zarovich (The Sovereign of Barovia)

![[strahd_von_zarovich.jpg\|strahd_von_zarovich.jpg]]

* **Race**: Vampire (Undead Humanoid)
* **Faction**: The High-Born of Barovia / House von Zarovich
* **Role**: Darklord of Barovia / Master of Castle Ravenloft
* **Appearance**: An aristocratic, tall figure with sharp, aristocratic features, pale alabaster skin, and piercing amber eyes that smolder with ancient malice. He wears high-collared black and crimson velvet garments adorned with silver thread, tailored armor plates of blackened steel, and a sweeping midnight-black cloak.
* **Personality**: Arrogant, calculating, tragic, and utterly ruthless. Centuries of imprisonment within Barovia have worn down his pride into a profound, smoldering exhaustion. He views himself as the absolute master of his valley, yet is cursed to watch his beloved Tatyana slip through his grasp across endless cycles of reincarnation.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Strahd is an apex tactician and eldritch warrior who commands wolves, swarms of bats, and shadows with a gesture. He never engages in brute slugfests; instead, he uses his spider-climb, mist form, and legendary mobility to harass, divide, and systematically dissect his foes while wearing them down with devastating necromancy and charm magic. *(Note: Following the party's cleansing of the Swamp Fane at Berez, Strahd has lost his planar immunity and absolute regenerative invulnerability, making him vulnerable to mortal steel and radiant divine power for the first time in centuries).*

* **Armor Class**: 17 (Natural Armor).
* **Hit Points**: 164 (19d8 + 76).
* **Speed**: 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (bat form).
* **Saving Throws**: Dex +9, Wis +7, Cha +9.
* **Skills**: Arcana +15, Athletics +9, Deception +9, Insight +7, Perception +12, Religion +10, Stealth +14.
* **Damage Resistances**: Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks.
* **Damage Vulnerabilities**: Radiant (due to the severed Swamp Fane anchor).
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned.
* **Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 22.
* **Languages**: Abyssal, Common, Draconic, Elvish, Infernal.
* **Challenge**: 15 (13,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5.
* **Shapechanger**: If Strahd isn't in running water or sunlight, he can use his action to polymorph into a Tiny bat, a Medium wolf, or a cloud of mist, or back into his true form.
* **Misty Escape**: When he drops to 0 hit points outside his resting place, Strahd transforms into a cloud of mist instead of falling unconscious, provided he isn't in sunlight or running water. He must reach his coffin in Castle Ravenloft within 2 hours to recover.
* **Regeneration**: Strahd regains 20 hit points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point and isn't in sunlight or running water. If he takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.
* **Spider Climb**: Strahd can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
* **Spellcasting**: Strahd is a 9th-level spellcaster (Spell Save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:.
* **Multiattack (Humanoid or Wolf Form Only)**: Strahd makes two attacks, only one of which can be a bite attack.
* **Unarmed Strike (Humanoid Form Only)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 9 (1d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus significant damage. If the target is a creature, Strahd can grapple it (escape DC 18) instead of dealing bludgeoning damage.
* **Bite (Bat or Humanoid Form Only)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing creature, or a creature that is grappled by Strahd, incapacitated, or restrained. *Hit*: 12 (2d6 + 5) piercing damage plus significant damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and Strahd regains hit points equal to that amount.
* **Charm**: Strahd targets one humanoid he can see within 30 feet. If the target can see Strahd, it must succeed on a saving throw against this magic or be charmed. The charmed target regards Strahd as a trusted friend to be heeded and protected.
* **Move**: Strahd moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Unarmed Strike**: Strahd makes one unarmed strike.
* **Bite (Costs 2 Actions)**: Strahd makes one bite attack.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Feast with the Party :** In Castle Ravenloft’s grand dining hall, Strahd received the adventurers not as petty trespassers, but as interesting planar anomalies. Weary of the centuries-long cycle, he made a shocking proposition: he offered his favor and an end to the dreaded "vein tax" in Barovia's villages in exchange for information regarding Tatyana’s current reincarnation, stating he was willing to give up his immunity because he was tired of his eternal prison.
* **The Fatal Ignorance:** Unknown to Strahd, the adventurers traveled to the Ruins of Berez, fought and killed his adoptive mother **Baba Lysaga**, severed her ritual chronal link to the three Hag Dragons, and deployed a **Planar Anchor** that permanently unhinged his domain's metaphysical shields. Strahd currently believes Baba Lysaga is merely secluded in her swamp; should he discover the party slain her and stole the **Crimson Weave**, his calm diplomacy will turn into an apocalyptic blood vengeance that will tear Barovia apart.
* **The Corrupted Weave:** Strahd's ancient blood magic had seeped into the Loom Fragment, turning it into the Crimson Weave. He willingly permitted the party to carry the corrupted fragment away, confident that the blood curse within it would bind them to his will—unaware that they were bound for the Colossal Forge in Metallos to burn the blood out of it.
