# Key NPCs - The Ironbound Covenant

The following figures make up the high command, judicial inquisitors, diplomatic assets, and secret inner circle of [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Ironbound Covenant\|The Ironbound Covenant]].

* * *

## Supreme Command

The singular authority guiding the endless crusade against [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Gods/God of the Plane of Infinite Hunger/Vorzath, the Maw Eternal\|Vorzath, the Maw Eternal]].

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Urdakael, the Unbroken\|Urdakael, the Unbroken]] — Forge-Herald of the Covenant, Living Doctrine, and Supreme Commander

* * *

## The Sealed Conclave (The Seven Seats)

The secret inner order that governs the Covenant's hidden strategies and guards the truth behind the First Forging.

* **Seat I — The Tempered**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Urdakael, the Unbroken\|Urdakael, the Unbroken]] — Forge-Herald; supreme field authority
* **Seat II — The Annealed**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Thessivex the Still\|Thessivex the Still]] — Master of Intelligence and Planar Strategist
* **Seat III — The Quenched**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Archivist Mauren Coldsteel\|Archivist Mauren Coldsteel]] — Keeper of the Bound Archive and First-Forged Chronicler
* **Seat IV — The Alloyed**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Vexiryn the Unbowed\|Vexiryn the Unbowed]] — Covenant Diplomatic Envoy to the Consortium and Pale Lodge
* **Seat V — The Drawn**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Halverak the Shorn\|Halverak the Shorn]] — Grand Paladin of the Mandate and Conclave Enforcer
* **Seat VI — The Cast**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Norath of the Leaving Flame\|Norath of the Leaving Flame]] — High Forge Judge, Theologian, and Administrator of Severance
* **Seat VII — The Questioned**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom(Player Friendly)/Player-Friendly/NPCs/Aeldris the Dim\|Aeldris the Dim]] — The Conclave Doubter and Voice of Conscience
