# **Sorrowsworn**

*“Creatures woven from the negative emotions that bleed into the Shadowfell—loneliness, hunger, frantic loss, blind rage, and wretched despair. They are not ghosts of the dead, but living scars of the living soul.”*

---

### **Overview**
**Sorrowsworn** are physical embodiments of emotional rot native to the deepest shadows of the Shadowfell. When mortals wander into the Plane of Shadow and surrender to hopeless despair, starvation, or maddening anger, the plane's negative essence reacts with their psychological ruin, spinning their agony into terrifying, emaciated monstrosities.

In the Shattered Loom cosmology, Sorrowsworn arise where threads of fate fray and tangle without snapping entirely. They are the psychic friction of souls dragged toward oblivion.

---

### **The Five Manifestations of Dread**

1. **The Lonely**: Towering, weeping humanoids with elongated arms ending in cruel harpoon-like bone hooks. They desperately crave company, yet their very touch drains warmth and isolates victims in cold sorrow.
2. **The Hungry**: Gaunt horrors with jaws that unhinge from collarbone to belly, driven by endless ravenous starvation. They are drawn to the scent of blood, greed, and gluttony.
3. **The Lost**: Twisted, hunchbacked creatures with spiked iron arms, permanently disoriented. They seek to ensnare travelers and drag them into the suffocating fog.
4. **The Angry**: Bulky juggernauts with two-pronged bladed arms that burn with cold, black flame. They fly into blind furies whenever struck, fueled by the rage of betrayal.
5. **The Wretched**: Small, scampering terrors that travel in packs, chewing on scraps of despair left behind by other Sorrowsworn.

## Behavior & Known Combat Traits

* **Sorrowful Presence**: Any creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Lonely must succeed on a saving throw or take severe damage and have its speed halved until the start of its next turn.
* **Harpoon Arm**: The Lonely’s arms extend like weighted chains, allowing it to hook distant targets and pull them into its despairing embrace.
* **Multiattack**: The Lonely makes two Harpoon Arm attacks.
* **Harpoon Arm**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (2d10 + 3) piercing damage plus severe damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is pulled up to 30 feet toward the Lonely and grappled (escape DC 15).
* **Embrace of Desolation**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature grappled by the Lonely. *Hit*: 21 (4d8 + 3) necrotic damage. The target must make a saving throw, suffering 1 level of exhaustion on a failure.
* **Life Hunger**: If a creature within 60 feet of the Hungry regains hit points, the Hungry gains advantage on its attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
* **Ravenous Frenzy**: While within 10 feet of a bleeding creature or a creature with fewer than half its maximum hit points, the Hungry has advantage on melee attack rolls.
* **Multiattack**: The Hungry makes two attacks: one with its Gaping Bite and one with its Claws.
