# **Black Beast of Bedlam**

*“A shape pulled from the deepest nightmares of the lunatic ward—a shifting mass of oil-slick sinew, gaping maws, and flailing limbs that oozes from the asylum drains. When it breathes, it does not exhale air, but the collective screams of minds shattered by the dark mists.”*

---

### **Overview**
The **Black Beast of Bedlam** is an aberrant shadow monstrosity born from the confluence of intense psychic torment, dark planar mist, and corrupted ley energy. Lairing within the subterranean sewer drains and flooded asylum cells of abandoned sanitariums—most notoriously the **Bedlam Asylum** along the old road in Barovia—it feeds upon the delirium, terror, and despair of trapped souls.

In the Shattered Loom cosmology, the Black Beast of Bedlam is a byproduct of severed planar threads. When mortal minds snap under the pressure of cyclic dread, their unraveling mental filaments do not dissipate; they gather in stagnant drainage vaults and coalesce into this hungry, amorphous horror.

---

### **Lore & Nature**

#### **Drain Stalker & Slurping Terror**
The Beast travels through plumbing, flooded drainage ditches, and narrow sewer conduits by liquefying its shadowy mass. Victims first hear a wet, rhythmic slurping noise echoing through dry pipes and cracked porcelain basins, accompanied by a foul stench of sulfur, bile, and stagnant grave water. Those who peer into the drains hear raspy whispers urging them to *"Get out!"* moments before the beast erupts through the masonry.

#### **Psychic Pestilence**
Unlike mundane predators that hunt for flesh, the Black Beast feeds on emotional rupture. Its breath weapon is an oily, toxic miasma that chokes the lungs while projecting horrifying hallucinatory visions into the victim's mind, inducing dazed paralysis and hysteria.

#### **Connection to the Shattered Loom Campaign**
During **Session 5**, the adventurers (Rourke, Kyle, Charl, Radina, Adam, and Ethan) took shelter at the derelict Bedlam Asylum while en route to Castle Ravenloft during torrential rains. Investigating moans within the abandoned latrines, they disturbed the slumbering Black Beast. The monster ambushed the party in the courtyard with toxic breath and sweeping claws, dazing the druid Daniel before being blasted by Charl's divine smites, Adam's banishment hellfire, and Kyle's turret bombardment, which detonated the creature into an explosion of scorched bone and dissolving shadow.

---

### **Lair Actions & Asylum Hazards**
When encountered within an asylum, sanitarium, or sewer system, the Black Beast can manipulate the environment on initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties):
* **Drain Eruption**: Pressurized, toxic bilge sprays from 1d4 drain pipes within 60 feet. Creatures within 10 feet of a bursting pipe must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) acid damage and be knocked prone.
* **Echoes of the Mad**: Spectral screams of former inmates reverberate through the halls. Each creature within 60 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on attack rolls and concentration checks until the end of its next turn.
* **Shadow Slime**: The floor in a 20-foot radius becomes slick with living shadow-ichor. The area becomes difficult terrain. A creature entering or starting its turn in the area must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.

## Behavior & Known Combat Traits

* **Skills**: Athletics +8, Perception +5, Stealth +5
* **Amorphous Form**: The beast can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.
* **Shadow Stealth**: While in dim light or darkness, the beast can take the Hide action as a bonus action.
* **Sunlight Weakness**: While in sunlight, the beast has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws.
* **Stench of Bedlam**: Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the beast must succeed on a saving throw or be poisoned until the start of its next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the Stench of Bedlam for 24 hours.
* **Multiattack**: The beast makes three attacks: one with its Gaping Maw and two with its Shadow Claws.
* **Gaping Maw**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 18 (2d12 + 5) piercing damage plus severe damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the beast can't bite another target.
* **Shadow Claw**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.
