# Map & Geography of the Nine Hells (The Spindle of Absolute Tyranny)

![[the_nine_hells_map.jpg\|the_nine_hells_map.jpg]]

> _"The Nine Hells did not break when Telara's Loom shattered—they hardened. In the descent through the nine layers, every stone is calibrated, every river of blood is metered, and every soul is a thread spun into Asmodeus's counterfeit spindle. To walk Baator is to witness order so absolute, so suffocating, that even death is governed by a legally binding sub-clause."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Adapted Directly from D&D 5e Canon
> The Nine Hells within **Project Shattered Loom** directly integrates the official D&D 5e cosmology of **Baator** (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 64–66, *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus*, and *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes*):
> 
> * **The Nine Layers of Baator**:
>   1. **Avernus**: Uses the official **Map of Avernus** by Jared Blando (*Descent into Avernus*), including the Bronze Citadel, the River Styx, Fort Knucklebone, the High Hall of Elturel, and the Wandering Emporium.
>   2. **Dis**: The iron geometric labyrinth city of Dispater (*Manual of the Planes* / *Fires of Dis*).
>   3. **Minauros**: The endless sinking swamp and city of Mammon.
>   4. **Phlegethos**: The volcanic foundries and city of Abriymoch (Belial & Fierna).
>   5. **Stygia**: The frozen sea and icebergs encasing Levistus.
>   6. **Malbolge**: The sloping, mountainous avalanche prison of Glasya.
>   7. **Maladomini**: The ruined architectural monuments and quarries of Baalzebul.
>   8. **Cania**: The frozen glaciers and citadel of Mephistar (Mephistopheles).
>   9. **Nessus**: The deepest rift and citadel of Malsheem (Asmodeus).
> * **Loom Integration**: In this campaign, the Hells are the "Tension Weights" of reality. Devils do not merely torture souls; they harvest **Soul-Threads** to reforge reality into the **Infernal Spindle**—a mechanical counter-loom of absolute tyranny.

## The Nine Layers & Key Citadels

### 1. Avernus: The Shattered Frontier
* **Role**: The Planar Junkyard and front line of the Blood War against the Abyss.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Bronze Citadel**: Massive mobile fortress built from Loom debris, commanded by Archduke Zariel.
  * **The River Styx**: The corrupted memory-thread siphoning identities of all who cross.
  * **Fort Knucklebone**: Mad Maggie's scrap-fort and infernal war machine garage.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Zariel**, the Fallen Weaver.

### 2. Dis: The Iron Spindle
* **Role**: The Data-Vault of Order; city of suffocating geometric perfection.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Iron Tower**: Dispater's impregnable redoubt monitoring cosmic leylines for "chaos-leaks."
  * **The Bastion of Decrees**: Heavy basalt bureaucracy cataloging universal infractions.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Dispater**, Surveyor of Deviance.

### 3. Minauros: The Bog of Sinking Destinies
* **Role**: The cosmic waste compactor where discarded destinies and failed threads dissolve into mud.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Sinking City**: Mammon’s capital, constantly sinking under the weight of accumulated gold.
  * **The Scavenger's Dredges**: Pits where lost Loom-shards are pulled from the sludge.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Mammon**, Treasurer of Loom-Shards.

### 4. Phlegethos: The Smelting Pit
* **Role**: Foundries of obsidian and fire where lawless souls are melted into infernal iron.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Abriymoch**: Fortress city built inside a colossal caldera.
  * **The Anvils of Mandates**: Where the Codes of Nessus are forged into law-tablets.
* **Ruler**: **Belial & Fierna**, Smelters of Mandates.

### 5. Stygia: The Frozen Memory
* **Role**: Read-only backup archive of pre-Shattering reality preserved in eternal ice.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Tomb of Levistus**: Titanic iceberg imprisoning the archduke.
  * **Tantlin**: Freezing port city on the ice floes of the River Styx.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Levistus**, Frozen Archivist.

### 6. Malbolge: The Recursive Firewall
* **Role**: Shifting vertical logic-trap prison punishing chaotic entities.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Copper Fortress**: Glasya’s mobile palace moving like a needle across the crags.
  * **The Screaming Chasm**: A ravine echoing with the friction of shifting tectonic slopes.
* **Ruler**: **Archduchess Glasya**, Mistress of the Recursive Knot.

### 7. Maladomini: The Ruined Archive
* **Role**: Wasteland of decaying, abandoned architectural iterations.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Malagard**: Crumbling city of parchment and ink-stained ruins.
  * **The Quarry of Perfection**: Endless excavation pits seeking flawless stone.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Baalzebul**, Lord of the Failed Iteration.

### 8. Cania: The Void of Negation
* **Role**: Extreme thermal heat-sink buffering the Hells from cosmic entropy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Mephistar**: City of heated laboratories built atop glaciers.
  * **The Glacier of Kolyarut**: Deep trench where ancient inquisitors experiment on the "Final Thread."
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Mephistopheles**, Master of Hellfire.

### 9. Nessus: The Root Directory
* **Role**: The core command center of the Infernal Spindle.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Malsheem**: Citadel of Asmodeus suspended over the Serpent's Coil abyss.
  * **The Infernal Spindle**: Counterfeit loom transmitting signals of absolute tyranny across the multiverse.
* **Ruler**: **Lord Asmodeus**, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The River Styx
* **Description**: A dark, greasy torrent flowing through all nine layers, carrying the dissolved memories and identities of mortal souls.
* **Planar Trait**: Touching the water forces a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw; failure results in the effects of a permanent *Feeblemind* spell and complete memory loss.

### 2. Infernal Soul-Thread Contracts
* **Description**: Binding metaphysical documents signed in blood that transfer the mortal's thread directly to the devil's spindle.
* **Planar Trait**: Immune to dispelling; breaking an infernal contract triggers immediate soul-forfeit, damning the creature to Avernus upon death.
